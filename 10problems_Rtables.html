10 Problems: Categorical Data

Use to create the required tables and answer each question.
Enter numbers only, without words, percentage signs or commas.

Problem 1

Create a frequency table for Favourite.Subject.

How many pupils said that Maths was their favourite subject?

Problem 2

Create a frequency table for Transport.

How many pupils travel to school by bus?

Problem 3

Create a proportion table for House. Show the proportions as percentages.

What percentage of the pupils are in Stewart? Give your answer to one decimal place.

Problem 4

Create a frequency table for Year.Group.

How many pupils are in FII?

Problem 5

Create a proportion table for Favourite.Building. Show the proportions as percentages.

What percentage of pupils chose Playfair as their favourite building? Give your answer to one decimal place.

Problem 6

Create a contingency table of Year.Group and Transport, showing frequencies.

How many pupils in either FI or FII travel to school by car?

Problem 7

Create a contingency table of House and Transport, showing frequencies.

How many Stewart pupils travel to school either by bus or by walking?

Problem 8

Create a contingency table of Year.Group and Transport, including row totals.

What percentage of the FVI pupils walk to school? Give your answer to one decimal place.

Problem 9

Create a contingency table of Year.Group and Favourite.Subject, showing frequencies.

Find the total number of pupils who are either:

  • FII pupils whose favourite subject is Biology; or
  • FVI pupils whose favourite subject is Maths.

Problem 10

Create a contingency table of Favourite.Building and Favourite.Subject, including the relevant totals.

Of all the pupils whose favourite subject is Music, what percentage chose the Piping Hut as their favourite building? Give your answer to one decimal place.
