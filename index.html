Form III Exam Preparation

The first stage of your revision should include reviewing your notes and examples.

Making flashcards to summarise each topic is recommended.

If you are stuggling to find or access the questions to be used for revision, ask your teacher.

Practice Exam Papers

The Form III Maths exam consists of a Paper 1 (non-calculator) and a Paper 2 (calculator).
Each paper will last 45 minutes.

These practice exams will help you test your knowledge and understanding.
At the end of each practice paper are the answers and hints.
