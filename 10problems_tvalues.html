10 Problems: \(t\)-Test Critical Values

Use the Student's \(t\) distribution table in the Advanced Higher Statistics Data Booklet.
For each test, determine the degrees of freedom \(\nu\) and enter the required critical value to three decimal places.
For a two-tailed test, enter only the critical value having the same sign as the stated test statistic.

Problem 1

A baker claims that the mean mass of a particular type of loaf is \(800\) grams. A random sample of \(8\) loaves is used to investigate whether the mean mass is greater than \(800\) grams.

\[ \mathrm{H}_0:\mu=800 \qquad \mathrm{H}_1:\mu>800 \]

The test is conducted at the \(5\%\) significance level.

Test statistic: \(t=1.420\)

State the critical value to three decimal places.

Problem 2

A machine is intended to fill packets with a mean mass of \(250\) grams. A random sample of \(10\) packets is used to investigate whether the mean mass is less than \(250\) grams.

\[ \mathrm{H}_0:\mu=250 \qquad \mathrm{H}_1:\mu<250 \]

The test is conducted at the \(10\%\) significance level.

Test statistic: \(t=-1.100\)

State the critical value to three decimal places.

Problem 3

Test statistic: \(t=-2.050\)

State, to three decimal places, only the critical value having the same sign as the test statistic.
