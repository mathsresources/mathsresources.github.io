10-Problem Challenge: Compound Change

Solve each problem to unlock the next problem.
Enter numbers only, without units, pound signs, percentage signs or commas.

Problem 1

A savings account contains £11,200 on 15th March 2026. Its balance increases by 0.7% each month. Calculate the balance on 15th November 2026.

Problem 2

A nature reserve has a population of 14,800 rabbits at the beginning of 2027. The population decreases by 7% each year. Calculate the expected population at the beginning of 2032. Give your answer to the nearest hundred.

Problem 3

A camera is bought for £860. Its value depreciates by 12% each year. Calculate its value after 4 years.

Problem 4

A laboratory culture contains 7,200 cells at 8 am. The number of cells increases by 6% every hour. Calculate the number of cells in the culture at 3 pm. Give your answer to the nearest thousand.

Problem 5

A painting is valued at £2,400. Its value appreciates by 5.5% each year. Calculate its value after 3 years.

Problem 6

A tank contains 3,200 litres of water at 6 am. The amount of water decreases by 8% every hour. Calculate the amount of water remaining at 11 am. Give your answer to the nearest ten.

Problem 7

A car is valued at £31,500 on 1st March. Its value decreases by 3% each month. Calculate its value on 1st September.

Problem 8

A house is valued at £275,000 at the beginning of 2024. Its value increases by 2.8% each year. Calculate its value at the beginning of 2028.

Problem 9

A forest contains approximately 18,500 trees at the beginning of 2025. The number of trees increases by 4.5% each year. Calculate the expected number of trees at the beginning of 2031. Give your answer to the nearest hundred.

Final Room

A machine is initially worth £36,000. During the first 3 years, its value depreciates by 12% each year. During the next 2 years, its value depreciates by 8% each year.

Calculate the value of the machine after 5 years. Then calculate the overall percentage decrease in its value.

Submit the overall percentage decrease, giving your answer to three significant figures.
